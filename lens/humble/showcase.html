HUMBLE Companion — Amber CRT Mascot

Original retro-futurist CRT terminal guide made of warm amber glowing light. 1960s cartoon squash & stretch motion, rubber-hose limbs, and 7 expressive states.

State 1

Sleep

Screen off / gentle breathing bobbing, faint CRT scanlines & dimmed antenna ball (opacity 0.45).

State 2

Think

Isolation phase executing steps. Inquisitive head tilt, upward focused gaze & active antenna pulse.

State 3

Walk

Traversing screen towards action bounds. 1960s rubber-hose leg stride & rhythmic counter arm swings.

State 4

Point

Guiding developer attention. Radiant conical lantern beam & outstretched arm pointing at code.

State 5

Talk

Explaining instructions and decisions. 3-shape alternating speaking mouth & friendly gesturing arm.

State 6

Celebrate

Replay passed! Victory squash jump, arms high, blue flash & printed PROVEN receipt slip.

State 7

Worried

Break diagnosed or command failed. Screen tints hot pink (#ff2d87) with nervous shiver.
